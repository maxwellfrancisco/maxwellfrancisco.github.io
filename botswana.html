Tracking Fire along the Trans-Kalahari Highway in Botswana

Tools: ArcPy, Raster Algebra, ArcGIS Pro, Google Earth Engine, Canva.

Overview

This is an ongoing and in-progress project focused on analyzing patterns of burns and shrub encroachment on a stretch of the Trans-Kalahari Highway in Botswana. The goals of this project are to understand the impact of fire the Kalahari landscape, and any patterns with burn severity, frequency, seasonality, or interval using a combination of on-the-ground site observations and satellite imagery.



Research poster presented at the 2026 UT Geography Undergraduate Symposium.
